03
Building the tense

Have / has + V3

✅The situation

I’ve finished my project. It’s ready.

The project is ready. You are sharing the news now.

I / you / we / they have
he / she / it has+V3past participle
ФормаСтруктураПриклад
Ствердженняsubject + have/has + V3She has finished her project.
Запереченняsubject + haven’t/hasn’t + V3She hasn’t finished her project.
ПитанняHave/Has + subject + V3?Has she finished her project?
Коротка відповідьYes / No + subject + have/hasYes, she has. / No, she hasn’t.
Understand the rule

Here, have/has is an auxiliary verb. Use it to form questions and negatives. Regular verbs end in -ed: finish → finished. Irregular verbs may change: see → saw → seen.

Have/has тут — допоміжне дієслово. Питання й заперечення утворюємо з ним. Для правильних дієслів V3 закінчується на -ed: finish → finished. Для неправильних форма може змінюватися: see → saw → seen.

🔗I have → I’ve · We have → We’ve · She has → She’s
✅She’s finished her project. = She has finished her project.
📖read → read → read: V2 і V3 вимовляємо /red/.

Homework: put it all together

1. Discover two new patterns
Understand the rule

This is the first time + subject + have/has + V3 describes a new experience happening for the first time now.

It’s my first visit to an observatory. → This is the first time I’ve visited an observatory.

Після This is the first time вживаємо Present Perfect. Називаємо досвід, який уперше відбувається зараз.

Understand the rule

The best / most interesting … + subject + have/has ever + V3 compares this experience with all your experiences up to now.

🎬 This is the best film I’ve ever seen.

📖 It’s the most interesting book she’s ever read.

Найвищий ступінь прикметника + Present Perfect допомагає порівняти цей досвід з усім попереднім досвідом до зараз.

5. Your everyday experience

Choose one topic. Write 80–100 words as one connected story. Follow the four questions in order.

🍳 Make something to eat

Choose a sandwich, pancakes, a cake or another simple meal.

  1. What food have you made, and how many times have you made it?
  2. Think of one particular time. When and where did you make it? Who helped you?
  3. What ingredients did you use? What did you do, and how did it taste?
  4. What have you learned or improved since then? Is it the best or most interesting thing you’ve ever made? Why?

🎬 Watch a film or read a book

Choose one film, episode, comic or book you know well.

  1. What have you watched or read recently? Have you tried other stories in the same series?
  2. When did you watch or read this one? Where were you, and who was with you?
  3. What happened in one interesting scene? How did you feel about it?
  4. What have you remembered or learned from it? Is it the funniest, best or most interesting story you’ve ever watched or read? Explain.

🎮 Learn a small everyday skill

Choose a game, an app, a sport move or a creative activity.

  1. What skill have you tried to learn? How much progress have you made so far?
  2. When did you first try it? Where were you, and who showed you what to do?
  3. What did you do during one practice session? What went well or went wrong?
  4. What have you learned already, and what haven’t you mastered yet? Is it one of the most useful or enjoyable things you’ve ever learned?

🚶 Visit a place near home

Choose a park, a café, a shop, a library or a friend’s home.

  1. Which place have you visited? How many times have you been there?
  2. When did you last go there? Who did you go with, and how did you get there?
  3. What did you see or do on that visit? What did you enjoy or dislike?
  4. What has made this place special to you? Is it one of the nicest or most interesting places you’ve ever visited? Why?
Your four-part story

1. Experience up to now → Present Perfect.
2. One past occasion → Past Simple.
3. Actions and feelings then → Past Simple.
4. What is different now + your opinion → Present Perfect, including a superlative + ever.
